Pinto beans

Also called: pinto beans, wachtelbohnen

A hearty, substantial bean — filling and cooling for Pitta.

How Pinto beans affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Pinto beans on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Use sparingly Can raise Vata — go easy when Vata runs high.
Pitta Balancing Calms Pitta — favour it when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Neutral Broadly neutral for Kapha.

Taste: Sweet, Astringent  ·  Potency: Cooling (śīta)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Dry

When to go easy

Astringent like all beans, so it can be gas-forming for Vata — cook soft with digestive spices.

Property-derived. Not directly cited classically — classified from its taste, potency and qualities. Weigh it accordingly.
