Miso soup

Also called: miso soup, miso

A light, hydrating fermented-soybean broth — grounding and easy to digest.

How Miso soup affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Miso soup on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Balancing Calms Vata — favour it when Vata runs high.
Pitta Neutral Broadly neutral for Pitta.
Kapha Neutral Broadly neutral for Kapha.

Taste: Salty, Sweet  ·  Potency: Neutral  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Moist

When to go easy

Fermented and salty, so a spicy or very salty bowl can nudge Pitta.

Cautions

  • High Sodium
