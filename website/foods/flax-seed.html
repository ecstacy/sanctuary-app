Flax seed

Atasi·अतसी

Also called: alsi, linseed, leinsamen

Oily and warming — grounding for Vata, and gently laxative when ground.

How Flax seed affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Flax seed on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Balancing Calms Vata — favour it when Vata runs high.
Pitta Use sparingly Can raise Pitta — go easy when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Use sparingly Can raise Kapha — go easy when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Sweet, Astringent  ·  Potency: Heating (uṣṇa)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Heavy, Oily

When to go easy

Heating and heavy, so it adds to Pitta and Kapha.

Traditionally balanced with

Classical pairings that make Flax seed easier to digest or more balancing for the doshas.

  • Fennel seed
  • Coriander seed

In the classical texts

Flax seed is attested in the Charaka Saṃhitā (Sutrasthana 27) — Atasi is classical; oily, warming character is consensus..

Classically cited. Directly attested in the classical Ayurvedic corpus.
