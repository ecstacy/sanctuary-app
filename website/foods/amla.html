Amla

Amalaki·आमलकी

Also called: indian gooseberry, amalaki, nellikai

Foremost of the rasayana (rejuvenative) fruits — cooling, five-tasted, and classically held to balance all three doshas, Pitta most of all.

How Amla affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Amla on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Balancing Calms Vata — favour it when Vata runs high.
Pitta Balancing Calms Pitta — favour it when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Balancing Calms Kapha — favour it when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Sour, Astringent, Sweet, Bitter, Pungent  ·  Potency: Cooling (śīta)  ·  Post-digestive: Sweet  ·  Qualities: Light, Dry

In the classical texts

Amla is attested in the Charaka Saṃhitā (Sutrasthana 27) — Amalaki is strongly attested and genuinely tridoshic in the classical texts — the rare case where the three-dosha claim is the tradition's, not a derivation. The sour taste (not the dryness) carries the Vata pacification..

Classically cited. Directly attested in the classical Ayurvedic corpus.
