Coffee

Also called: kaffee, espresso, filter coffee

How Coffee affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Coffee on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Use sparingly Can raise Vata — go easy when Vata runs high.
Pitta Use sparingly Can raise Pitta — go easy when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Balancing Calms Kapha — favour it when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Bitter, Astringent  ·  Potency: Heating (uṣṇa)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Dry, Sharp

When to go easy

Sharp, drying and stimulating — it pushes Vata into restlessness and adds heat to Pitta. Its one merit is cutting Kapha heaviness.

Best time to eat it

Time of day: Morning

Cautions

  • Pregnancy
  • Anxiety
  • Acid Reflux
  • Insomnia

Property-derived. Not directly cited classically — classified from its taste, potency and qualities. Weigh it accordingly.
