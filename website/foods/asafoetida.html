Asafoetida

Hingu·हिङ्गु

Also called: hing, heeng, asant

The classical answer to gas and bloating — a pinch in cooking is what makes heavy legumes digestible, which is why it appears wherever dal does.

How Asafoetida affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Asafoetida on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Balancing Calms Vata — favour it when Vata runs high.
Pitta Use sparingly Can raise Pitta — go easy when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Balancing Calms Kapha — favour it when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Pungent  ·  Potency: Heating (uṣṇa)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Oily, Sharp

When to go easy

Sharp and heating: it adds to Pitta.

How to prepare Asafoetida

Always cooked briefly in fat first; raw, it is harsh.

In the classical texts

Asafoetida is attested in the Charaka Saṃhitā (Sutrasthana 27).

Classically cited. Directly attested in the classical Ayurvedic corpus.
