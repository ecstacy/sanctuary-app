Watermelon

Also called: tarbooz, wassermelone, melon

Cooling and watery — one of the best foods for summer Pitta and heat.

How Watermelon affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Watermelon on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Neutral Broadly neutral for Vata.
Pitta Balancing Calms Pitta — favour it when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Use sparingly Can raise Kapha — go easy when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Sweet  ·  Potency: Cooling (śīta)  ·  Post-digestive: Sweet  ·  Qualities: Heavy, Moist

When to go easy

Heavy and watery, so it adds to Kapha, and it sits poorly with other foods — best eaten alone.

Best time to eat it

Time of day: Midday  ·  Season: Summer
