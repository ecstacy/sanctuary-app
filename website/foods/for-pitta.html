Ayurvedic food guide

Best foods for Pitta

Pitta is hot, sharp, oily and intense. To cool it, favour cooling, sweet and mildly bitter or astringent foods — and ease off hot, sour, salty and fried ones.

These lists come from the same reviewed dataset the app uses. Your ideal plate depends on your whole constitution and the season — find your dosha →.
