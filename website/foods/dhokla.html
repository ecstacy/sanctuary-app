Dhokla

Also called: dhokla, khaman

A steamed, fermented gram-flour cake — light and spongy.

How Dhokla affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Dhokla on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Use sparingly Can raise Vata — go easy when Vata runs high.
Pitta Neutral Broadly neutral for Pitta.
Kapha Neutral Broadly neutral for Kapha.

Taste: Sweet, Sour, Astringent  ·  Potency: Neutral  ·  Post-digestive: Sweet  ·  Qualities: Light, Soft

When to go easy

Gram flour is astringent, so it can be a little Vata-provoking.

Property-derived. Not directly cited classically — classified from its taste, potency and qualities. Weigh it accordingly.
