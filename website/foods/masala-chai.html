Masala chai

Also called: chai, masala tea, spiced tea

Warm, spiced and grounding — the milk and warming spices settle Vata.

How Masala chai affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Masala chai on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Balancing Calms Vata — favour it when Vata runs high.
Pitta Use sparingly Can raise Pitta — go easy when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Neutral Broadly neutral for Kapha.

Taste: Sweet, Pungent, Astringent  ·  Potency: Heating (uṣṇa)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Moist

When to go easy

Black tea and warming spices can add to Pitta; caffeinated.

Cautions

  • Caffeine

Practical flag: black-tea base carries caffeine; sugar varies by preparation.
