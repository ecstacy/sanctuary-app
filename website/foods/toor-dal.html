Toor dal

Adhaki·आढकी

Also called: arhar, pigeon pea, tuvar, tur dal, split pigeon pea

The everyday dal — light, astringent, cooling for Pitta and drying for Kapha.

How Toor dal affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Toor dal on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Use sparingly Can raise Vata — go easy when Vata runs high.
Pitta Balancing Calms Pitta — favour it when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Balancing Calms Kapha — favour it when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Astringent, Sweet  ·  Potency: Cooling (śīta)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Dry

When to go easy

Dry and gas-forming, as pulses are, so it unsettles Vata without help.

Best time to eat it

Time of day: Midday
