Chana dal

Chanaka·चणक

Also called: split bengal gram, split chickpea, gram dal

The split form of chickpea — dry, astringent, settling Pitta and reducing Kapha.

How Chana dal affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Chana dal on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Use sparingly Can raise Vata — go easy when Vata runs high.
Pitta Balancing Calms Pitta — favour it when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Balancing Calms Kapha — favour it when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Sweet, Astringent  ·  Potency: Cooling (śīta)  ·  Post-digestive: Pungent  ·  Qualities: Light, Dry, Rough

When to go easy

Notably gas-forming and drying; the hardest of the common dals for Vata.

Best time to eat it

Time of day: Midday

Traditionally balanced with

Classical pairings that make Chana dal easier to digest or more balancing for the doshas.

  • Asafoetida
  • Cumin
  • Ginger (fresh)
  • Ghee

In the classical texts

Chana dal is attested in the Charaka Saṃhitā (Sutrasthana 27) — Same classical substance as whole chickpea (Chanaka); split and skinned it is a touch easier..

Classically cited. Directly attested in the classical Ayurvedic corpus.
