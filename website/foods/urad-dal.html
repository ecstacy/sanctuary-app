Urad dal

Masha·माष

Also called: black gram, black lentil, urad, dal makhani

The heaviest and most building of the pulses — genuinely nourishing and strengthening where mung is light and recovering.

How Urad dal affects your doshas

In Ayurveda a food's effect depends on your constitution. This is the lens the app uses to suggest what to favour and ease off.

Effect of Urad dal on each dosha
DoshaSuitabilityNotes
Vata Balancing Calms Vata — favour it when Vata runs high.
Pitta Use sparingly Can raise Pitta — go easy when Pitta runs high.
Kapha Use sparingly Can raise Kapha — go easy when Kapha runs high.

Taste: Sweet  ·  Potency: Heating (uṣṇa)  ·  Post-digestive: Sweet  ·  Qualities: Heavy, Oily, Unctuous

When to go easy

Heating and heavy: it adds to both Pitta and Kapha, and is slow to digest in quantity.

Best time to eat it

Time of day: Midday

How to prepare Urad dal

Long-cooked to make the heaviness manageable. That changes how the food is experienced, not its intrinsic properties — the ratings above already describe the dal itself.

Traditionally balanced with

Classical pairings that make Urad dal easier to digest or more balancing for the doshas.

  • Asafoetida
  • Ginger (fresh)
  • Cumin
  • Black pepper

In the classical texts

Urad dal is attested in the Charaka Saṃhitā (Sutrasthana 27).

Classically cited. Directly attested in the classical Ayurvedic corpus.
