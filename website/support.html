Frequently asked

What is a dosha, and why does the app ask about it?

In Ayurveda, your dosha (vata, pitta, kapha) describes your constitution — patterns of energy, body, and temperament. The Sanctuary uses it as a lens to pick poses and sequencing that suit you, rather than serving everyone the same routine.

How is Today's Practice chosen?

Each day the app composes a fresh session from its pose library using your dosha profile, your recent check-ins, and the time of day — energizing in the morning, unwinding in the evening. Same ritual, never the same session twice.

Which languages are supported?

English, German (Deutsch), and Hindi (हिन्दी) — interface, content, and voice guidance. Switch anytime in Profile → Language.

How do practice reminders work?

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device — enable them in Profile → Notifications and pick your time. There's also an optional streak saver and an evening wind-down nudge; each can be toggled separately, and Android lets you silence any category in system settings.

What's included in Sanctuary Plus?

Plus unlocks the deeper Ayurvedic layer — vikriti history and insights, and premium protocols — on top of the free daily practice. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from Profile.

How do I export or delete my data?

Profile → "Download my data" exports everything as JSON. Profile → "Delete my account" permanently removes your account and all data — no email required, effective immediately.

Is the app medical advice?

No. The Sanctuary supports general wellbeing and is not a medical product. If you're pregnant, injured, or managing a condition, check with a professional first — and always stop a pose that hurts.
