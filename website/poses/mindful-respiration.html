Mindful Respiration

Pranayama

A two-minute reset for the nervous system. Slow, even breathing engages the vagus nerve, drops the heart rate, and pulls you out of fight-or-flight — the fastest evidence-based intervention for acute stress and anxiety.

Benefits

  • Calms anxiety quickly
  • Lowers stress response
  • Sharpens focus
  • Anchors the wandering mind
