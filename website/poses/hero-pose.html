Hero Pose

Virasana·वीरासन·vīrāsana

Also called: Hero's Seat

Virasana is the deeper-knee cousin of Vajrasana — the sit bones are between the heels rather than on them. Most users need a block under the seat for years before reaching the floor. The quad stretch is unique; few other poses target it from this angle.

Benefits

  • Stretches the quadriceps and the front of the ankles in a way no other pose reaches
  • Stable seat for meditation and pranayama, especially for users with tight cross-legged seats
  • Like Vajrasana, safely practiced after meals
  • HYP-named — connects modern users to one of the foundational seats of the original tradition

How to practise Hero Pose

  1. Kneel on the floor with knees together.
  2. Place your feet wider than your hips. The tops of the feet flat on the floor, toes pointing back.
  3. Sit between your heels — your sit bones land on the floor or on a yoga block.
  4. Most users need a block under the seat for years. Start tall, lower the block over weeks.
  5. If the tops of the feet don't reach the floor, roll a thin blanket under the front of the ankles.
  6. Rest your hands on your thighs.
  7. Lengthen the spine, soften the shoulders.

Suggested hold: 120s · Breath: Natural

Who should avoid or modify this pose

  • Don't practice with knee injury, meniscus tear, or recent knee surgery
  • Skip with ankle injury
  • Pregnant women should sit on a high block or bolster between the heels
  • Avoid if Vajrasana already strains the knees

If you're pregnant, injured, or managing a condition, check with a qualified teacher or clinician before practising.

Modifications

  • Sit on a yoga block placed between the heels — start tall, lower the block over weeks
  • Place a folded blanket between the calves and thighs to reduce knee compression
  • Roll a blanket under the front of the ankles if they don't reach the floor
  • Build duration gradually — 30 seconds, then a minute, then longer

Traditional source: Hatha Yoga Pradipika 1.21 — Named in the Hatha Yoga Pradipika Ch. 1 alongside other meditation seats. The Sanskrit "vīra" means hero, warrior.
