Reclined Hero Pose

Supta Virasana·सुप्त वीरासन·supta vīrāsana

Also called: Reclining Hero

Supta Virasana is a deep quad stretch in disguise — most users don't realize how short their quads have become from chronic sitting until they try this pose. The bolster behind the spine is non-negotiable for most bodies. The 1-3 minute hold is when the fascial release actually lands.

Benefits

  • Deep stretch through the quadriceps, hip flexors, and front of the ankles — combination unmatched by other poses
  • Mild backbend opens the chest
  • Excellent post-workout pose for runners, cyclists, and seated workers
  • Reclined version is more accessible for chest-opening than Camel or Bow

How to practise Reclined Hero Pose

  1. Start in Virasana — kneeling with knees together, feet wider than hips, sitting between the heels (use a block under the seat if needed).
  2. Place your hands on the floor behind you and slowly lean back onto the elbows.
  3. Pause here. If the knees, hips, or quads complain, stay on the elbows or come back up.
  4. If accessible, lower all the way down onto the back. Place a bolster lengthwise behind the spine for support.
  5. Arms can rest by the sides, palms up, or extend overhead on the bolster.
  6. Hold for 1-3 minutes. The release is in the duration — don't rush.
  7. To exit: press up onto the elbows first, then to the hands, then sit up.

Suggested hold: 120s · Breath: Natural

Who should avoid or modify this pose

  • Don't practice with knee injury, meniscus tear, or recent knee surgery
  • Skip with ankle injury
  • Pregnant women shouldn't practice — the back-bend depth isn't safe
  • Skip with herniated disc or severe lower-back injury
  • Avoid if Virasana itself strains the knees — that's the prerequisite

If you're pregnant, injured, or managing a condition, check with a qualified teacher or clinician before practising.

Modifications

  • Stay propped on the elbows instead of going all the way down
  • Place a bolster lengthwise behind the spine — most users need this
  • Place a folded blanket under the knees if they hurt
  • Sit on a high block in Virasana before reclining — tilts the pelvis forward

Traditional source: Modern hatha 1.21 — Modern reclined variant of the Hatha Yoga Pradipika 1.21 Virasana. The reclined version intensifies the quad and hip-flexor stretch.
