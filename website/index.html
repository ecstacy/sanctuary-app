How it works

Three steps to a practice that fits.

A short Ayurvedic assessment maps your constitution — vata, pitta, or kapha — the lens every recommendation is made through.

Full video demonstrations with calm, step-by-step voice guidance. Roll out the mat and press play — the sequencing is done for you.
